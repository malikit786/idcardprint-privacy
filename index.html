Privacy Policy

App: ID Card Print, Scan & PDF (com.thebestdeveloper.idcardprint)
Developer: The Best Developer
Last updated: November 24, 2026

ID Card Print, Scan & PDF is designed with your privacy first. The app operates completely offline on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information, scanned ID card images, or PDFs on any external server.

1. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect personal data, analytics, usage telemetry, advertising identifiers, location data, or crash reports containing user documents. All scanning, cropping, enhancement, and PDF generation happen locally on your device.

2. Permissions Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to capture images of ID cards and documents when you initiate a scan. Camera frames are processed locally on-device for real-time edge detection and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Gallery / Photo Picker: When you choose to import an existing photo, the app uses Android's built-in System Photo Picker so you only grant access to the exact photos you select.

3. Local Storage & User Control

When you tap Save, generated printable PDF sheets and preview images are stored strictly inside the app's private local storage on your device. You can view, rename, print, share (only when you explicitly tap Share), or permanently delete any saved file inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

This app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no cloud sync services. Files leave your device only when you explicitly choose to print or share a PDF using your device's system Print or Share menu.

5. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in a future release, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at: github.com/malikit786/myidscan2.
