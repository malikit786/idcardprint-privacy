On-Device ID Card Processing

Privacy Policy

App: ID Card Print, Scan & PDF (com.thebestdeveloper.idcardprint)
Developer: The Best Developer
Last updated: November 25, 2026

ID Card Print, Scan & PDF is built with your privacy first. All ID card scanning, cropping, enhancement, PDF generation, and saved document storage happen 100% locally on your device. We never upload, view, collect, sell, or share your scanned ID cards or PDFs.

1. Information We Collect & Push Notifications

  • Scanned Images & PDFs: Never collected or transmitted. All documents stay strictly inside your device's private local storage.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications): We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) solely to deliver optional app update alerts and reminders to open the app. For this purpose, Firebase generates an anonymous app-instance notification token (a device/app identifier) encrypted in transit to route notifications to your device. No personal identity, account info, or scanned images are ever linked to this token. You can disable notifications at any time in your Android device settings.

2. Permissions Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely to capture images of ID cards and documents when you initiate a scan. Camera frames are processed locally on-device for real-time edge detection and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional permission on Android 13+ used to display push notifications sent via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Gallery / Photo Picker: When you choose to import an existing photo, the app uses Android's built-in System Photo Picker so you only grant access to the exact photos you select.

3. Local Storage & User Control

When you tap Save, generated printable PDF sheets and preview images are stored strictly inside the app's private local storage on your device. You can view, rename, print, share (only when you explicitly tap Share), or permanently delete any saved file inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data and invalidates the Firebase notification token.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

This app contains no advertising SDKs and no user accounts. We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging only for delivering push notifications. Files leave your device only when you explicitly choose to print or share a PDF using your device's system Print or Share menu.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general audiences aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in a future release, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
